Work Stations

Bind a Bud to a Workstation and it works a field on its own — tilling, planting, felling, digging, mining — as long as it's fed. Here's exactly how to set one up and tune it.

Place the Workstation, then open it — it has a card slot, a secondary input slot (seeds/saplings/ore, depending on role), and a fuel slot with 8 output slots.

Binding a Bud

Drop the matching Bud Card into the Workstation's card slot — the Bud spawns (or teleports in, if already active) directly at the station and switches into its Working state. Each Workstation only accepts the Bud whose role matches: Keyleth for Farming, Gronkh for Lumbering, Veri for Mining. Removing the card unbinds and despawns the Bud again.

Fuel & resting

A bound Bud needs fuel in the Workstation's fuel slot to keep working — each accepted food item covers FuelDurationSeconds (120s by default) of work. Out of fuel, or nothing left to do in the field, and the Bud switches to its Resting sub-state (idle pose, near or on the station) until fuel is added or new work appears.

RoleAccepted fuel
FarmingPlant_Fruit_Berries_Red

Edit allowedFuel in work/recipes.yml to change what each role accepts.

Farming — Keyleth

Scans the field for soil to till, plants an accepted seed if one sits in the input slot, waters and fertilizes tilled soil, and harvests ripe crops into the output slots.

Till→ Plant→ Water→ Fertilize→ Harvest

Accepted seeds (allowedSeeds: FARMING in work/recipes.yml): Aubergine, Carrot, Cauliflower, Chilli, Corn, Cotton, Lettuce, Onion, Potato, Pumpkin, Rice, Tomato, Turnip, Wheat — plus each crop's Eternal variant.

Lumbering — Gronkh

Prepares the soil under each tree spot with a shovel first (see Tree roots below), tills and plants a sapling if one sits in the input slot, and fells any full-grown tree in the field — drops (including Life Essence) go straight into the output slots, no loot to pick up off the ground.

Prepare Soil→ Till→ Plant→ Fell

Accepted saplings (allowedSeeds: LUMBERING): Ash, Aspen, Azure, Bamboo, Beech, Birch, Bottletree, Cedar, Crystal, Dry, Fir, Fire, Frostwood, Gumboab, Jungle, Maple, Oak, Palm, Palo, Redwood, Sallow, Spiral, Willow.

Mining — Veri

Digs holes scattered across the field that grow into minable stone over time, and works the field's ore-bearing main nodes for a steady, renewable ore yield.

Dig→ Wait for growth→ Mine

Accepted ore (allowedSeeds: MINING): Adamantite, Cobalt, Copper, Gold, Iron, Mithril, Onyxium, Prisma, Silver, Thorium.

Tree roots

Hytale's tree prefabs don't only grow upward — from the third growth stage on they also place root blocks below the sapling, and the engine only accepts a growth stage if every block it needs is either air or soil. Hit stone under a sapling and the placement is rejected silently: the growth timer keeps running, the tree never visibly changes, and once the timer finishes the block entity is deleted — the tree is then frozen forever, waiting doesn't help.

That's why Gronkh performs a soil-preparation step with a shovel before tilling: at each tree spot he turns every non-soil, non-air block into Soil_Dirt, both at the surface and TreeRootDepth blocks down, across a TreeRootRadius column. Already-soil blocks are left alone, and a spot with something already planted on it is skipped, so a growing tree's own roots are never dug back up.

The defaults (depth 4, radius 1) cover the common trees. Species with deeper roots need larger values, or they'll stall exactly as described above:

⚠️ Mind the cost before raising these: the cleared volume is (2 × radius + 1)² × depth blocks per tree spot. The defaults clear 36 blocks; Fir's 6/4 clears 486; Wisteria's 9/10 clears 3969. If you only plant Oak, leave the defaults alone — the cheaper alternative to a large radius is restricting allowedSeeds: LUMBERING in work/recipes.yml to species your setting actually covers.

work/recipes.yml holds the item-level side of things — allowedSeeds / allowedFuel per role, tillable/diggable block lists, and treeGrowthStageSeconds (game-seconds per sapling growth stage; {default: 10000} ships out of the box). Both files follow the same copy-on-first-start convention as everything else in the mod: the packaged default is only written if the file is missing, so your edits are never overwritten.
